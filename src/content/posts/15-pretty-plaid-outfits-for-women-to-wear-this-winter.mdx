Plaid outfits are the easiest way to look put-together this winter, because the pattern does all the work for you. A single plaid piece — a skirt, a shacket, a scarf or a coat — adds colour, texture and personality to the plain knits and dark basics we all live in during the cold months.

In this guide, you will find 15 pretty plaid outfits for women to wear this winter, covering plaid skirts, plaid shackets, plaid scarves, plaid coats and plaid trousers. Every look comes with a step-by-step styling plan and a simple outfit formula you can copy with pieces already in your closet, plus practical tips on fit, layering and keeping warm without losing the shape of the outfit.

## 1. The Red Plaid Mini Skirt with Knee-High Boots

A red tartan mini skirt is the boldest way to start your plaid winter wardrobe, and it works best when the rest of the outfit stays calm. Begin with opaque black tights — 80 denier or higher — so the skirt reads polished rather than summery. Add a cream chunky knit sweater, front-tucked so your waist is defined, and finish with knee-high black leather boots that meet the hem of the skirt with no gap of bare leg.

Layer a black wool coat over the top on the coldest days, and let it hang open so the red plaid still shows. Keep jewellery simple: small gold hoops and one delicate necklace are enough, because the skirt is the star here.

Formula: Red plaid mini skirt + cream chunky knit + black tights + knee-high black boots + black wool coat.

Tip: If your skirt sits high on the waist, choose a sweater that ends at the hip so the front tuck looks intentional, not bulky.

## 2. The Green Plaid Shacket Over Straight-Leg Jeans

The plaid shacket is the hardest-working layer in a winter wardrobe: warm like a jacket, easy like a shirt. Start with a plain white T-shirt and straight-leg blue jeans, then wear a green and navy plaid shacket open over the top like a light jacket. Tan suede ankle boots warm up the palette and stop the look from feeling too dark.

This is a true everyday outfit — school runs, coffee dates, weekend errands. On colder mornings, swap the T-shirt for a thin thermal or a fine-knit roll neck in cream, and the shacket still fits over it comfortably. For more cold-weather layering ideas, see our [22 chic winter outfit ideas for women](https://outfitedits.com/posts/22-chic-winter-outfit-ideas-for-women/).

Formula: White T-shirt + green plaid shacket + straight-leg jeans + tan suede ankle boots.

Tip: Buy your shacket one size up if you plan to layer knits underneath; the shoulders should sit flat, not pull.

![Woman wearing a green and navy plaid shacket over a white T-shirt and jeans in a winter park](/images/content/15-pretty-plaid-outfits-for-women-to-wear-this-winter/2.webp)

## 3. The Oversized Plaid Blanket Scarf with a Camel Coat

If you own one plaid piece this winter, make it an oversized blanket scarf. Start with a camel wool coat, black skinny jeans and black ankle boots, then wrap a large red, cream and navy plaid scarf around your neck twice and let the ends drape down the front. The scarf adds all the pattern and colour the outfit needs.

Choose a scarf at least 140 cm long so it wraps properly, and fold it in half lengthways before looping it so it sits thick and cosy rather than flat. A plaid scarf also works over a puffer jacket on snowy days.

Formula: Camel wool coat + black skinny jeans + black ankle boots + oversized red plaid blanket scarf.

Tip: Keep the scarf's colours in the same family as your coat — red plaid loves camel, grey plaid loves black, green plaid loves brown.

![Woman wearing a camel coat with a large red plaid blanket scarf on a snowy street](/images/content/15-pretty-plaid-outfits-for-women-to-wear-this-winter/3.webp)

## 4. The Black-and-White Plaid Midi Dress Under a Camel Coat

A black-and-white plaid midi dress is the most elegant plaid piece you can own, and it needs almost nothing else. Start with the dress itself — a shirt dress with a collar and buttons down the front — then add a thin black belt to mark your waist. Wear it under a long camel coat left open, so the plaid shows in a long stripe down the centre.

Black tights and pointed black heeled boots keep the line long and lean, and a small black handbag finishes the look without competing.

Formula: Black-and-white plaid midi shirt dress + thin black belt + camel coat + black tights + pointed black heeled boots.

Tip: If your dress is A-line, skip the belt and let the shape fall straight — a belt only flatters a dress with a defined waist seam.

## 5. Grey Plaid Wide-Leg Trousers with a Cream Knit

Grey plaid trousers are the quiet, grown-up way to wear the trend. Start with a high-waisted wide-leg pair in grey and burgundy plaid, then add a cream oversized knit sweater with a front tuck so the waistband and the pattern stay visible. White sneakers keep the outfit relaxed and daytime-ready, while a small tan crossbody bag picks up the burgundy in the plaid.

Comfortable, warm and far more interesting than plain black trousers. If sneakers feel too casual, swap them for tan loafers and the same outfit becomes smart enough for a lunch out.

Formula: Grey plaid wide-leg trousers + cream oversized knit + white sneakers + tan crossbody bag.

Tip: Wide-leg trousers need a shoe with a little height or a clean white sneaker — flat ballet shoes disappear under the hem and shorten your legs.

## 6. The Brown Plaid Blazer with Leather Leggings

A brown plaid blazer turns the simplest winter base into a polished outfit. Start with a black turtleneck and black faux-leather leggings, then add an oversized brown and beige plaid blazer. Black heeled ankle boots lengthen the leg, and a structured black tote keeps the look smart enough for the office or a city day out.

The blazer should end at mid-hip, covering the waistband of the leggings, so the outfit reads as tailored rather than gym-like. If blazers are your thing, our guide on [12 ways to style a black blazer](https://outfitedits.com/posts/12-ways-to-style-a-black-blazer/) has more layering combinations to try.

Formula: Black turtleneck + brown plaid blazer + black leather leggings + black heeled ankle boots.

Tip: Push the blazer sleeves up to just below the elbow — it shows the turtleneck cuffs, adds shape and stops an oversized blazer from swallowing you.

## 7. The Red Plaid Pleated Maxi Skirt with a Cable Knit

A plaid pleated maxi skirt brings movement and festive colour to cold days. Start with a red and green plaid pleated maxi skirt, then add a chunky oatmeal cable-knit sweater tucked in at the front. Brown leather ankle boots ground the outfit, and a small shearling-trimmed bag adds a cosy winter texture.

Pleats do the styling for you — they move as you walk and catch the light — so keep the top simple and let the skirt shine. This look is lovely for Christmas markets, winter walks and holiday photos, and it stays warm because the long skirt blocks the wind.

Formula: Red plaid pleated maxi skirt + oatmeal cable-knit sweater + brown ankle boots + shearling bag.

Tip: Pleated skirts crease easily when you sit, so hang yours after wearing instead of folding it, and steam the pleats back into shape before your next outing.

Add one small colour echo — a red handbag, a red lip, or a red beanie — and the outfit looks deliberate instead of accidental. ## 9. The Navy Plaid Belted Shirt Dress with Knee-High Boots

Button it all the way up for a smarter look, or undo the top two buttons and layer a thin white T-shirt underneath for a more relaxed feel.

Formula: Navy plaid shirt dress + tan leather belt + black tights + brown knee-high boots + cream scarf.

Tip: Match your belt to your boots, not your dress — tan with tan keeps the outfit connected from waist to toe.

Formula: Black-and-white plaid puffer jacket + dark skinny jeans + chunky black snow boots + black beanie + red plaid scarf.

## How to Mix Plaid Without Clashing: A Quick Checklist

Run through this quick checklist before you leave the house:

- **One plaid at a time** — unless you are wearing a matching co-ord set, let one plaid piece be the star and keep everything else solid.
- **Match the scale** — if you do mix patterns, pair a large plaid with a tiny stripe or a small polka dot, never two large checks together.
- **Repeat one colour** — pick one colour from the plaid and repeat it in your shoes, bag or scarf so the outfit feels connected.
- **Keep the base simple** — black, cream, camel, grey and denim are the best partners for any plaid.
- **Check the mirror at arm's length** — if the pattern fights with your top, swap the top for a plain one before you go.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do you style plaid in winter without looking too casual?

Start with one plaid piece and build the rest from polished basics. A plaid pencil skirt with a turtleneck and blazer looks office-ready, while a plaid coat over black trousers looks elegant. The key is structure: tailored shapes, good tights and proper boots lift plaid from casual to smart.

### What colours go best with red plaid outfits?

Red plaid pairs beautifully with black, cream, camel, navy and grey. Black grounds the red and makes it look bold, cream softens it for daytime, and camel adds warmth. Avoid pairing red plaid with bright green or orange, which can look festive in the wrong way outside of Christmas.

### Can you wear plaid to the office in winter?

Yes, plaid works at work when you choose a small-scale check in muted colours like grey, navy or brown. A plaid pencil skirt or a plaid blazer with tailored trousers reads as professional. Keep the rest simple and structured, and save large, bright tartans for weekends.

### How do I keep warm in a plaid skirt outfit during winter?

Layer thermal tights under opaque black tights, and choose knee-high or over-the-knee boots so no skin shows between the hem and the boot. A long wool coat adds warmth without hiding the outfit, and a plaid scarf keeps the cold out. Fleece-lined tights are warmest on freezing days.

### What shoes go best with plaid outfits for women?

Knee-high boots suit plaid mini skirts and shirt dresses, ankle boots work with plaid trousers and shackets, and heeled pumps polish up a plaid pencil skirt for the office. For casual plaid looks, white sneakers or tan loafers keep things relaxed. Match shoes to one colour in the plaid.

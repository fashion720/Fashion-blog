## Common Styling Questions

### How do I choose the right colour palette for my basic outfits?

Start by identifying three neutral colours you naturally gravitate toward, such as navy, cream, or charcoal. These shades mix easily, reducing the time spent coordinating pieces. Adding one accent colour through accessories can provide interest without overwhelming your look, keeping the overall aesthetic grounded and easy to manage throughout the year.

### What is the best way to add texture to simple outfits?

Combine different fabric weights to create depth without needing extra colours. Pair smooth silk or satin with chunky knits or layer denim over soft cotton. This approach keeps your outfit visually engaging while maintaining a simple, flattering silhouette. Always consider how the fabrics feel against your skin for long term comfort.

### Should I prioritize layering for seasonal transitions?

Layering is essential for maintaining comfort as temperatures fluctuate. Start with a fitted base layer, like a light cotton tee, and add structured pieces like blazers or trench coats. This allows you to adjust your outfit throughout the day without losing your polished look, ensuring you stay warm and look intentional.

### How can I finish my outfits with the right accessories?

Keep accessories minimal to maintain a clean look. A simple belt can define your waist, while classic jewellery like small hoops or a watch adds subtle sophistication. Choose footwear that aligns with the formality of your clothes, ensuring your overall ensemble feels balanced from head to toe for any occasion.

### What are the best tips for making basic outfits look flattering?

Focus on your proportions by balancing fitted items with relaxed pieces. Tucking in your top can emphasize your waist, while choosing the right pant length ensures a clean line. Prioritize garments that fit your body well, as proper tailoring is the most effective way to ensure any outfit looks intentional and stylish.

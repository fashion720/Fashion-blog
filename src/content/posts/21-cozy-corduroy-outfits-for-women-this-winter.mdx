These 21 cozy corduroy outfits show you how to wear it beyond the obvious — trousers, skirts, jackets, dresses, and even a full suit — without looking like a 1970s costume.

You will learn which wale width suits which piece, which shoes balance corduroy's weight, and how to keep it from going baggy by midday. Every outfit has a formula you can copy and a tip that saves you from corduroy's classic mistakes. ### Your Corduroy Buying and Care Checklist

Before you buy, or before you wear the corduroy you already own, run through this list:

- **Fine wale for trousers, wide wale for jackets** — fine ribs drape better on legs, wide ribs give jackets structure.
- **Buy snug, it relaxes** — corduroy stretches at the knee, elbow, and waist by midday.
- **A little stretch helps** — 2% elastane keeps trousers and skirts holding their shape.
- **Wash inside out, cold water** — hot water shrinks corduroy and dulls the pile.
- **Hang, don't fold** — folding crushes the ribs and leaves creases that are hard to steam out.
- **Brush the pile** — a soft clothes brush restores the nap after wear and lifts dust from the ribs.
- **Keep a lint roller handy** — dark corduroy shows light fibres, and light corduroy shows dark ones.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is corduroy warm enough for winter?

Yes — corduroy is warmer than denim because the ribbed pile traps air against your skin, which is why corduroy trousers, skirts, and jackets work through the coldest months. For the coldest days, layer thermal leggings under corduroy trousers and a thermal base under a corduroy dress. Wide-wale corduroy is warmer than fine-wale, because the deeper ribs hold more air.

### How do I stop corduroy trousers going baggy at the knees?

Buy them snug in the morning, because corduroy relaxes as you wear it, and choose a blend with 2% elastane, which helps the fabric spring back. Hang the trousers after wear instead of folding them, and wash them inside out in cold water — hot water and tumble drying make the bagging worse. A trouser press at the knee also helps the fabric recover its shape.

### Can I wear corduroy to work?

Yes — a camel corduroy blazer with tailored trousers, or a navy corduroy suit with a white shirt, reads as professional tailoring with texture. Keep the blazer structured at the shoulder, the trousers straight-legged, and the rest of the outfit plain. Avoid corduroy overalls and bucket hats for the office, and save the playful pieces for weekends.

### What shoes go with corduroy trousers?

Tan loafers suit wide-leg corduroy trousers best, ankle boots suit straight-leg trousers, and white sneakers work with corduroy joggers and overalls. For skirts and dresses, knee-high boots close the gap between hem and shoe, while chunky ankle boots suit A-line and mini skirts. The shoe should balance corduroy's weight — a delicate flat can look lost under wide-leg corduroy.

### How do I wash corduroy without ruining it?

Wash corduroy inside out in cold water on a gentle cycle, and air-dry it hanging rather than tumble drying, because heat shrinks the fabric and crushes the pile. Brush the pile with a soft clothes brush once it is dry to restore the nap, and store corduroy hanging on a wide hanger rather than folded, so the ribs do not crease.

The best après-ski outfits do two jobs at once: they warm you up fast after a cold day on the mountain, and they look polished enough for the lodge bar, the fireplace, and every photo in between. You do not need a whole new wardrobe to pull it off. Most of these looks start with pieces you already own — a chunky knit, a warm pair of leggings, a good coat — layered in a smarter way.

Below you will find 16 cozy après-ski outfits for women, each with step-by-step styling instructions and a simple outfit formula you can copy in minutes. Whether you are heading straight from the slopes to hot chocolate, dressing up for a lodge dinner, or spending the evening fireside, there is a look here for every moment of your ski trip.

## 1. Chunky Knit and Shearling Boots

![Woman in a chunky cream knit sweater and shearling boots on a snowy ski lodge terrace](/images/content/16-cozy-apres-ski-outfits-for-women/1.webp)

This is the classic après-ski outfit for a reason: it is warm, effortless, and photographs beautifully against the snow. Start with a thermal base layer in a neutral shade, then pull on an oversized chunky knit sweater in cream, oatmeal, or camel. Tuck just the front into dark skinny jeans so the proportions stay neat, and finish with tan shearling-lined snow boots that keep your feet warm on icy lodge steps.

Add a knit beanie in a matching neutral and a plaid scarf you can unwrap indoors. Keep jewelry minimal — small gold hoops are enough — and carry a crossbody bag so your hands stay free for a mug of cocoa.

Formula: Oversized chunky knit + dark skinny jeans + shearling boots + knit beanie.

Tip: Choose a sweater with a slightly dropped shoulder and ribbed cuffs; the relaxed shape layers easily over thermals without looking bulky.

## 2. Faux Fur Vest and Leggings

![Woman in a cream faux fur vest and black leggings beside a lodge fireplace](/images/content/16-cozy-apres-ski-outfits-for-women/2.webp)

A faux fur vest turns the simplest base layers into a full après-ski look in seconds, which makes it perfect for the rush from the slopes to the lodge. Start with a black long-sleeve thermal top and black fleece-lined leggings, then layer a cream or taupe faux fur vest over the top. The vest adds instant texture and warmth around your core, where you lose the most heat after skiing.

Slip on brown shearling ankle boots and add a thin belt at the waist if the vest feels too boxy. 

Formula: Faux fur vest + black thermal top + fleece-lined leggings + shearling ankle boots.

Tip: Keep the layers underneath fitted and smooth so the vest sits flat. Bulky knits under a fur vest add width exactly where you do not want it.

## 3. Oversized Turtleneck and Puffer

![Woman in an oversized ivory turtleneck and sage puffer jacket on a snowy mountain path](/images/content/16-cozy-apres-ski-outfits-for-women/3.webp)

When the temperature drops after sunset, this is the outfit that keeps you warm without making you look like you just came off the lift. Start with a thermal camisole, then pull on an oversized ivory turtleneck sweater. Layer a cropped puffer jacket in sage green, navy, or black over it — cropped, so the sweater hem shows and the outfit keeps its shape.

Pair the look with light-wash straight-leg jeans and white winter sneakers or low-profile snow boots. Roll the turtleneck collar once so it frames your face instead of swallowing it, and add a slim crossbody bag in a neutral tone.

Formula: Oversized turtleneck + cropped puffer + straight-leg jeans + winter sneakers.

Tip: If your puffer is long instead of cropped, add a belt over the jacket at your natural waist. It defines your shape and stops the layers from looking like one big rectangle.

## 4. Fair Isle Sweater and Jeans

![Woman in a red Fair Isle sweater and jeans outside a snow-dusted ski lodge](/images/content/16-cozy-apres-ski-outfits-for-women/4.webp)

Nothing says ski lodge quite like a Fair Isle sweater, and this look works from the first coffee of the morning to drinks by the fire. Start with a red and cream or navy and cream Fair Isle knit, and pair it with dark straight-leg jeans. The pattern does all the visual work, so keep everything else simple: tan leather ankle boots, a burgundy knit beanie, and a small leather crossbody bag.

Tuck the sweater in at the front for a cleaner line, and let the sleeves fall just past your wrists for that relaxed, lived-in feel. 

Formula: Fair Isle sweater + dark straight-leg jeans + tan ankle boots + knit beanie.

Tip: Match your beanie to one of the smaller colors in the Fair Isle pattern, not the main one. It ties the outfit together without looking too matched.

## 5. Quilted Ski Jacket and Knit Dress

![Woman in a navy quilted ski jacket and burgundy knit dress on a lodge deck](/images/content/16-cozy-apres-ski-outfits-for-women/5.webp)

This outfit is the fastest way to look dressed up after a day in ski gear. Start with a burgundy or forest-green ribbed knit midi dress and black tights. Layer a navy quilted ski jacket over it, worn open so the dress shows through, and add brown knee-high suede boots to keep your legs warm on the walk to dinner.

A thin leather belt at the waist sharpens the silhouette, and a small structured handbag finishes the look. Keep your hair down or in a low bun so the jacket collar does not fight with it.

Formula: Quilted ski jacket + ribbed knit midi dress + black tights + knee-high suede boots.

Tip: Choose a knit dress with a little stretch and a fitted bodice. A loose dress under a quilted jacket hides your shape and adds bulk at the waist.

## 6. Plaid Scarf and Wool Coat

![Woman in a camel wool coat and plaid scarf on a snowy village street](/images/content/16-cozy-apres-ski-outfits-for-women/6.webp)

A plaid scarf is the easiest way to add a festive, lodge-ready touch to a coat you already own. Start with a cream sweater and dark jeans as your base, then wrap a red and green or camel and black plaid scarf around your neck, letting one end fall longer than the other. Layer a camel wool wrap coat over everything and finish with tan ankle boots.

Tie the coat at the waist to create shape, and keep your gloves in a matching neutral so the scarf stays the star. 

Formula: Plaid scarf + camel wool coat + cream sweater + dark jeans + ankle boots.

Tip: When you sit down indoors, drape the scarf over the back of your chair instead of leaving it bunched around your neck. It keeps the outfit looking neat all evening. ## 7. Teddy Coat and Snow Boots

![Woman in a cream teddy coat and snow boots outside a log cabin lodge](/images/content/16-cozy-apres-ski-outfits-for-women/7.webp)

A teddy coat is basically a wearable blanket, which is exactly what you want after hours in the cold. Start with a beige knit sweater and black leggings, then layer a long cream teddy coat over the top. Add tan lace-up snow boots with good grip, and finish with a pom-pom beanie in cream or camel.

Keep the layers underneath slim so the coat can do the talking. A small crossbody bag worn under the coat keeps your phone and lift pass safe, and thin leather gloves are easier to manage than bulky ski gloves once you are off the mountain.

Formula: Long teddy coat + beige knit sweater + black leggings + lace-up snow boots.

Tip: Teddy fabric attracts lint, so keep a small lint roller in your lodge bag. A quick roll before dinner photos takes ten seconds and makes a real difference.

## 8. Cashmere Lounge Set

![Woman in a heather-grey cashmere lounge set by a lodge fireplace](/images/content/16-cozy-apres-ski-outfits-for-women/8.webp)

Some après-ski moments call for zero effort: you have showered, you are warm, and you are not leaving the lodge. A matching cashmere lounge set is the answer. Start with a relaxed heather-grey cashmere hooded sweater and wide-leg knit pants in the same shade. The matching set looks intentional, not sloppy, which is the whole trick.

Add cream fuzzy slippers and a mug of hot chocolate, and you are done. 

Your lodge lounge checklist:

- Matching knit set in a neutral shade
- Fuzzy slippers or shearling ankle boots
- A soft robe coat for stepping outside
- A good book, a blanket, and a warm drink

Formula: Matching cashmere lounge set + fuzzy slippers + robe coat for outdoors.

Tip: Wash cashmere on a cold, gentle cycle and dry it flat. A well-cared-for cashmere set lasts for years and only gets softer with wear.

## 9. Velvet Leggings and Long Cardigan

![Woman in black velvet leggings and a long oatmeal cardigan by a lodge window](/images/content/16-cozy-apres-ski-outfits-for-women/9.webp)

Velvet leggings bring a soft, dressy texture to an otherwise casual lodge outfit, and they are far warmer than they look. Start with a cream thermal top, then add black velvet leggings and an oversized oatmeal long cardigan that falls to mid-thigh or below the knee. The long cardigan covers the hip area and balances the fitted leggings.

Finish with tan suede ankle boots and a delicate gold necklace. If the cardigan feels too plain, add a thin belt at the waist to give the outfit shape and a little polish.

Formula: Velvet leggings + cream thermal top + long open cardigan + suede ankle boots.

## 10. Cable-Knit Dress and Knee-High Boots

![Woman in a cream cable-knit dress and knee-high boots on a lodge terrace](/images/content/16-cozy-apres-ski-outfits-for-women/10.webp)

A cable-knit dress is one of the most flattering après-ski pieces you can own, because the texture adds interest without any extra effort. Start with a cream or oatmeal cable-knit sweater dress that hits at or just above the knee. Add black tights and black knee-high leather boots, which lengthen your legs and keep you warm at the same time.

Layer a camel scarf loosely around your neck and carry a small crossbody bag. 

Formula: Cable-knit sweater dress + black tights + knee-high boots + camel scarf.

Tip: If your dress is on the shorter side, choose boots that hit just below the knee and tights in the same color as the boots. The unbroken line makes your legs look longer.

## 11. Fleece-Lined Leggings and Parka

![Woman in a forest-green parka and fleece-lined leggings walking in fresh snow](/images/content/16-cozy-apres-ski-outfits-for-women/11.webp)

This is the most practical outfit on the list, built for real cold and real walking in snow. Start with a thermal base layer, then add black fleece-lined leggings — the fleece lining traps heat in a way regular leggings simply cannot. Layer an oversized hooded parka in forest green, burgundy, or black over the top, and finish with insulated snow boots that have a grippy sole.

Add a cream knit beanie and thin touchscreen gloves so you can use your phone for photos without taking your gloves off. A small backpack works better than a handbag here, since it keeps your hands free on icy paths.

Formula: Fleece-lined leggings + thermal base layer + hooded parka + insulated snow boots.

Tip: Check the parka's hood before you buy: a hood that fits over a beanie without pulling is worth its weight in gold on a windy mountain evening.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What should I wear for après-ski if I am not skiing?

You do not need ski gear to enjoy après-ski. A chunky knit sweater, warm leggings or jeans, and snow boots are all you need for the lodge. Focus on warm, comfortable layers that look polished, like a faux fur vest or a teddy coat, and you will fit right in.

### How do I stay warm at après-ski without looking bulky?

Layer thin, warm pieces instead of one thick one. A thermal base layer under a sweater, fleece-lined leggings, and a well-fitted coat trap heat without adding bulk. Choose a cropped or belted outer layer to keep your shape visible.

### Can I wear jeans for après-ski?

Yes, jeans work well for après-ski as long as they are warm enough. Dark straight-leg or skinny jeans with a thermal layer underneath are the most practical choice. Pair them with snow boots or shearling ankle boots, and avoid light-wash jeans in wet snow, since they show damp patches.

### What shoes are best for après-ski?

Snow boots with a grippy sole are the safest choice for walking on ice and slush around the lodge. Shearling-lined boots, insulated ankle boots, and knee-high boots all work, depending on your outfit. Save heeled boots for indoor evenings at the lodge bar or restaurant.

### What should I pack for a ski trip if I only want one après-ski outfit?

Pack one chunky knit sweater, dark jeans, fleece-lined leggings, a warm coat, snow boots, a beanie, and a scarf. That combination covers the lodge, the bar, and a snowy stroll. Add a sequin top or a satin blouse if you have a lodge dinner planned, and you are set for the whole trip.

From chunky knits to sequin tops, these 16 cozy après-ski outfits prove that the best part of a ski trip often starts after the last run. Pick your favorite formula, pack your warmest layers, and enjoy every fireside moment.

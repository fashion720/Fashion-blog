New Year's Eve is the one night of the year when your hair has to do three jobs at once: look incredible in photos, survive hours of dancing, and still hold up at midnight when the countdown hits. These 15 New Year's Eve hairstyles are built for exactly that — every look below comes with the exact steps, products, and hold tricks to get you through the night without a single touch-up panic.

You will find sleek buns that stay put through a countdown hug, soft waves that photograph like a dream, and party updos that work with the dress you already own. Each style includes a formula you can copy and a tip from real party-night experience, so you can pick your look with confidence.

Start with clean, day-old hair if you can — freshly washed hair is slippery and refuses to hold a pin. And if you are still choosing your outfit, our [20 New Year's Eve outfit ideas for women](https://outfitedits.com/posts/20-new-years-eve-outfit-ideas-for-women/) pair perfectly with every hairstyle here, from sequin minis to velvet gowns.

## 1. The Sleek Low Chignon

A low chignon is the most reliable New Year's Eve hairstyle there is, because it looks expensive, photographs beautifully from every angle, and will not move no matter how long the night runs. Start by smoothing your hair back with a boar-bristle brush and a pea-sized amount of styling cream, then gather it at the nape and twist it into a low knot. Pin it with U-shaped hairpins rather than bobby pins — they grip a twisted bun far better and disappear into the hair.

For the glass-smooth finish, run a flat iron over the top layer before you gather it, and mist the finished style with a strong-hold hairspray from about 30 cm away. The [chignon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chignon_(hairstyle)) has been a party classic for over a century for a reason: it frames the face, shows off earrings, and never competes with your dress. Pull out two whisper-thin strands at the front if you want it softer.

Formula: smoothing cream + boar-bristle brush + low twist + U-shaped pins + strong-hold hairspray.

Tip: build the chignon on second-day hair and add a little dry shampoo at the roots first — the grip is twice as good as on freshly washed hair.

## 2. Old Hollywood Waves

Old Hollywood waves are the definition of New Year's Eve glamour — deep, glossy, side-parted waves that catch the light in every photo. The secret is a 32 mm curling iron and patience: curl every section in the same direction, away from your face, and clip each curl up to cool while you work through the rest. Once the hair is completely cool, brush it out gently with a paddle brush until the curls melt into one continuous wave.

Set the waves with a flexible-hold spray, not a crunchy one, and finish with a pea-sized drop of shine serum smoothed over the surface. This style works best on hair that reaches at least the shoulders, and it pairs beautifully with a red lip and a [glamorous holiday dress](https://outfitedits.com/posts/15-glamorous-holiday-dress-outfits-for-women/). Keep the part deep on one side — that is what gives it the vintage drama.

Formula: 32 mm curling iron + pin curls to cool + paddle brush + flexible-hold spray + shine serum.

Tip: never brush the waves out while they are still warm — you will lose the shape in seconds. Let every curl cool completely first.

![Woman with old Hollywood waves hairstyle and a deep side part at a New Year's Eve party](/images/content/15-new-years-eve-hairstyles-for-a-night-out/2.webp)

## 3. The High Party Ponytail

A high ponytail is the most fun New Year's Eve hairstyle on this list, and it is also the most practical — your hair stays off your neck while you dance, and the height photographs like a lift for your whole face. Gather your hair at the crown, not the back of the head, and secure it with a snag-free elastic wrapped twice. Tease the underside of the ponytail at the base for volume, then wrap a small section of hair around the elastic and pin it underneath.

Curl the ponytail itself in large sections with a 25 mm iron for bounce, and mist the roots with a texturising spray before you gather so nothing slips. This look works brilliantly with statement earrings and a [stylish Christmas party outfit](https://outfitedits.com/posts/17-stylish-christmas-party-outfits-for-women/) that shows your shoulders. If your hair is fine, add a clip-in ponytail extension for extra swing.

Formula: texturising spray + high crown placement + snag-free elastic + hair-wrapped base + curled ponytail.

Tip: tilt your chin down while you gather the hair — it pulls the ponytail higher and stops the sides from sagging by midnight.

## 4. Soft Romantic Updo with Face-Framing Pieces

This is the updo for anyone who wants their hair up but hates the severe, scraped-back look. Start by curling the whole head in loose waves, then gather the hair loosely at the back and pin it in soft folds, leaving the front sections out. The key is imperfection: pin the bun in three or four loose loops rather than one tight knot, and let the pins hide inside the folds.

Pull the face-framing pieces out after the bun is secure, curl them lightly, and let them fall just in front of your ears. A light-hold spray keeps everything soft without stiffness, and a few pearl pins scattered through the bun add a festive touch. This style suits every dress length and works especially well if your hair is layered, since the layers create natural texture in the bun.

Formula: loose curls + low loose bun + face-framing pieces + pearl pins + light-hold spray.

Tip: pin the bun with the pins pointing in different directions — crossed pins hold a soft updo far longer than parallel ones.

## 5. The Voluminous Blowout

A big, bouncy blowout is the ultimate "I woke up like this, but better" New Year's Eve hairstyle — and it is far easier than it looks once you know the order. Rough-dry your hair until it is about 80 percent dry, then work in sections with a large round brush, lifting each section up and away from the root while the dryer nozzle points down the hair shaft. Roll each finished section into a large velcro roller and let it cool while you do the rest.

Once everything is cool, remove the rollers, flip your head upside down, and shake the roots out with your fingers — never a brush, or you will flatten the volume you just built. A root-lifting spray applied to damp hair before you start makes a visible difference, and a cool shot from the dryer on each section locks the shape in. This look pairs beautifully with a [rich burgundy winter outfit](https://outfitedits.com/posts/17-rich-burgundy-winter-outfits-for-women/) and gold jewellery.

Formula: root-lifting spray + large round brush + velcro rollers + cool shot + finger shake-out.

Tip: dry the front sections last so they are the freshest and bounciest when you walk out the door — they frame your face in every photo.

## 6. Glitter-Parted Sleek Straight Hair

If your hair is naturally straight, lean into it — a glass-smooth, centre-parted style with a glitter part line is one of the most photographed New Year's Eve hairstyles of recent years. Flat iron your hair in small sections, then smooth a tiny amount of shine serum over the top layer. The magic is the part: use a fine-tooth comb to draw a razor-straight centre part, then press cosmetic glitter gel or fine glitter along the part line with a small flat brush.

Keep the glitter to the part only — a thin line reads chic, while glitter scattered everywhere reads messy by midnight. A strong-hold spray on the lengths keeps flyaways down, and tucking the hair behind your ears shows off your earrings. This style is at its best on hair that falls past the shoulders, and it needs almost no maintenance once you are out, which is exactly what you want on a long night.

Formula: flat iron + shine serum + centre part + glitter gel along the part + strong-hold spray.

Tip: apply the glitter gel with a clean lip brush for a precise line, and seal it with a light mist of hairspray so it does not flake onto your dress.

## 7. The Textured Top Knot

A textured top knot is the cool-girl answer to a New Year's Eve updo — it looks effortless, keeps your hair completely out of the way, and takes about five minutes. Gather your hair into a high ponytail, then backcomb the ponytail gently for texture before wrapping it loosely around its own base. Pin it with U-shaped pins as you go, and pull out a few pieces around the hairline for softness.

The trick is not to make it perfect: a slightly messy knot with visible texture looks deliberate and modern, while a smooth ballerina bun is a different look entirely. A texturising spray through the lengths before you wrap gives the knot grip and fullness, and a sparkly hair cuff around the base turns it into a party style. This works on medium to long hair and survives dancing better than almost any other updo here.

Formula: texturising spray + high ponytail + backcombed wrap + U-shaped pins + sparkly hair cuff.

Tip: wrap the knot loosely on purpose — a tight wrap slips down the elastic as the night goes on, while a loose, pinned wrap stays put.

## 8. Hollywood Side-Swept Curls

Side-swept curls are Old Hollywood waves' more relaxed cousin — all the glamour, none of the precision. Curl your hair in large sections with a 32 mm iron, alternating directions this time for a softer, more natural fall. Once the curls are cool, sweep everything over one shoulder and pin it in place just behind your ear with two bobby pins crossed in an X.

The pin placement matters: hide them under the top layer of hair so the sweep looks like it falls that way on its own. A flexible-hold spray keeps the curls moving instead of freezing them, and a single statement earring on the exposed side balances the asymmetry. This style is ideal if your hair is thick, because the weight of the sweep keeps it in place all night without constant checking.

Formula: 32 mm iron + alternating curl directions + deep side sweep + crossed bobby pins + flexible-hold spray.

Tip: pin the sweep slightly higher than you think — gravity pulls it down over the first hour, and starting high keeps it in place until midnight.

![Woman with Hollywood side-swept curls over one shoulder at a New Year's Eve party](/images/content/15-new-years-eve-hairstyles-for-a-night-out/8.webp)

## 9. The Braided Crown Updo

A braided crown is the most romantic New Year's Eve hairstyle here, and it photographs beautifully from behind — which matters, because someone will take a photo of you from behind at some point. Start with two French braids, one on each side, beginning at the temples and working towards the back. Cross them over each other at the nape, tuck the ends underneath, and pin generously.

Pull the braids gently apart with your fingers before you pin them — this "pancaking" makes them look twice as thick and much more festive. A few face-framing curls soften the front, and small pearl or crystal pins pushed into the braids catch the light. This style holds for hours because the braids are anchored to your head, so it is a great choice if you plan to dance until 2 a.m.

Formula: two French braids + pancaked for thickness + crossed at the nape + pearl pins + face-framing curls.

Tip: braid on slightly dirty hair with dry shampoo at the roots — clean hair slips out of braids, but day-old hair grips and holds the pancaked shape.

## 10. Bouncy Curtain-Bang Blowout

If you already have curtain bangs, New Year's Eve is their moment — a bouncy blowout with the bangs swept open frames your face like a spotlight. Blow-dry the bangs first, while they are at their wettest, using a round brush to curve each side away from your face. Then blow out the rest of your hair in sections, rolling the ends under for bounce, and finish the whole head with large velcro rollers at the crown for lift.

The bangs are the make-or-break detail: they need to be dry and shaped before the rest of the hair, because once they air-dry flat they will not behave. A small amount of styling cream smoothed over the bangs after drying keeps them piecey rather than fluffy. If you are still deciding on fringe, our [25 curtain bangs hairstyles for every face shape](https://outfitedits.com/posts/25-curtain-bangs-hairstyles-for-every-face-shape/) will help you find your cut before the big night.

Formula: round-brush bangs first + section blowout + velcro rollers at crown + styling cream on bangs + light-hold spray.

Tip: never blow-dry curtain bangs from above with the nozzle pointing down — it flattens them. Dry them with the brush pulling out and away from your face instead.

## 11. The Chic French Twist

The French twist is the most elegant New Year's Eve hairstyle on this list, and it is simpler than it looks — it is one smooth roll, not a complicated braid. Gather your hair low at the back, twist it upwards against your head, and tuck the ends inside the roll. Pin along the seam with U-shaped pins, working from the bottom up, and smooth the surface with a brush and a little styling cream.

A French twist works best on medium to long hair with some length to tuck, and it shows off a bare back or a halter neckline like nothing else. Add a decorative comb or a crystal clip along the seam for a festive finish, and keep the front sleek with a centre or side part. This is the style to choose if your dress is dramatic — the hair stays quiet and lets the outfit do the talking.

Formula: low gather + upward twist + tucked ends + U-shaped pins along the seam + decorative comb.

Tip: practise the twist once before the night — it takes two tries to learn and thirty seconds to do once you have it, so a dry run saves party-night stress.

## 12. Half-Up Twisted Crown

A half-up twisted crown gives you the best of both worlds: your hair is up and out of your face, but the length is still down and moving. Take a section from each temple, twist each one back towards the crown, and cross them over each other at the back of your head. Pin the twist in place, then let the rest of your hair fall in soft waves below.

Curl the lengths first with a 25 mm iron so the bottom half has movement, and leave a few face-framing pieces loose at the front. A small crystal barrette over the join hides the pins and adds a New Year's Eve sparkle. This style suits every hair length past the shoulders and is especially flattering if you are wearing a high neckline, since it keeps the front open and airy.

Formula: curled lengths + two twisted temple sections + crossed at the back + crystal barrette + face-framing pieces.

Tip: twist the sections away from your face, not towards it — twists that roll backwards sit flatter and hold longer than twists that roll forwards.

## 13. The Glossy Wet-Look Bob

Short hair deserves a party moment too, and the glossy wet-look bob is it. Work a generous amount of strong-hold gel through damp hair, comb it back or into a deep side part, and let it set — do not touch it while it dries, or you will break the shine. Once it is set, smooth a drop of shine serum over the surface for a mirror finish.

The [bob cut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_cut) has been reinvented for every decade, and the wet-look version is its most party-ready form: it frames the jawline, shows off earrings, and needs zero maintenance once you are out. Tuck one side behind your ear for asymmetry, and keep the rest sleek. This look pairs beautifully with a [classic wool coat](https://outfitedits.com/posts/16-classic-wool-coat-outfits-for-women/) over your party dress on the way to the venue.

Formula: strong-hold gel on damp hair + comb into place + air-dry untouched + shine serum + one side tucked.

Tip: use gel, not oil — oil goes greasy by midnight, while gel sets and holds the shape until you wash it out.

## 14. Loose Mermaid Waves with Sparkle Clips

Loose mermaid waves are the most relaxed New Year's Eve hairstyle here — long, flowing, barely-there waves that look like you spent the day at the beach and the evening at a party. Wrap large sections around a 32 mm wand, leaving the last two inches of each section straight, and let the waves cool before you touch them. Brush through very gently with your fingers, not a brush, so the waves stay defined rather than turning into one big cloud.

The party detail is the clips: place two or three small crystal or pearl clips on one side, just above the ear, in a neat row. They catch the light in photos and keep the hair off your face while you dance. A sea-salt spray through the lengths before curling adds the texture that makes mermaid waves look lived-in instead of salon-perfect.

Formula: 32 mm wand + straight ends + finger brush-through + sea-salt spray + crystal clips on one side.

Tip: leave the ends straight on purpose — curled ends make the waves look done and formal, while straight ends keep them soft and modern.

![Woman with loose mermaid waves and sparkle clips at a New Year's Eve party](/images/content/15-new-years-eve-hairstyles-for-a-night-out/14.webp)

## 15. The Classic Ballerina Bun with a Crystal Barrette

End the night with the most reliable party hairstyle ever made: the classic ballerina bun, high on the crown, smooth as glass, finished with a crystal barrette. Brush the hair up into a high ponytail, twist it around its base, and pin it flat so the bun sits tight against the head. Smooth the surface with a brush and styling cream, and fix every flyaway with a toothbrush sprayed with hairspray — it is the oldest trick in the book and it works.

The crystal barrette is what makes it New Year's Eve rather than ballet class: place it just above or below the bun so it catches the light when you turn your head. Pair the bun with bold earrings and a [faux fur coat](https://outfitedits.com/posts/15-luxe-faux-fur-coat-outfits-for-women/) over your dress, and you have a look that will still be perfect in the 2 a.m. photos.

Formula: high ponytail + twisted bun + styling cream + toothbrush-and-hairspray flyaway fix + crystal barrette.

Tip: build the bun on hair that has been blow-dried smooth first — a bun built on wavy hair will always look lumpy, no matter how much you smooth it.

### Your New Year's Eve Hairstyle Checklist

Before the night starts, run through this list so nothing surprises you at 11 p.m.:

- **Second-day hair** — wash the day before, not the day of, so pins and braids actually grip.
- **Dry shampoo at the roots** — adds grip and volume, and buys you an extra hour of freshness.
- **U-shaped pins, not just bobby pins** — they hold twists and buns far better and stay invisible.
- **A travel-size hairspray in your bag** — one quick mist in the bathroom at midnight resets everything.
- **Extra pins and one spare elastic** — someone always needs one, and it is usually you.
- **Test your style once before the night** — a five-minute dry run beats a 9 p.m. panic.
- **A silk scarf for the taxi home** — protects the style from wind and keeps it photo-ready until you are inside.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best New Year's Eve hairstyle if I have to dance all night?

An updo is your safest choice — a low chignon, a braided crown, or a textured top knot will survive hours of dancing without falling. The key is building it on second-day hair with dry shampoo at the roots, and pinning with U-shaped pins rather than bobby pins. Avoid styles that rely on curls alone, since sweat and movement will drop curls long before midnight.

### How do I make my New Year's Eve hairstyle last until midnight?

Three things: start with day-old hair, use a strong-hold hairspray misted from 30 cm away, and carry a travel-size spray in your bag for one reset at 11 p.m. Pin styles with crossed pins, and never touch a curl until it has cooled completely. A light mist of hairspray over the finished style, rather than a heavy blast, holds better because it does not weigh the hair down.

### Can I do a New Year's Eve hairstyle on short hair?

Yes — a glossy wet-look bob, a sleek side part with a crystal clip, or a half-up twist all work beautifully on short hair. The wet-look bob is the easiest party style for a bob or lob because it needs gel and a comb, not heat, and it stays perfect all night. Add one statement accessory, like a crystal barrette, to make it feel festive rather than everyday.

### What hairstyle photographs best on New Year's Eve?

Styles with shine and structure photograph best: Old Hollywood waves, a sleek low chignon, and a glossy wet-look bob all catch light and read clearly in photos. Avoid very dark, very flat styles in dim party lighting — a little shine serum or a sparkly accessory gives the camera something to catch. Face-framing pieces also help, since they soften the face in flash photos.

### Should I wash my hair on New Year's Eve?

No — wash it the day before. Freshly washed hair is too slippery for pins, braids, and updos, and curls drop out of it much faster. If your roots feel oily on the day, use dry shampoo at the roots and brush it through. The only exception is a sleek straight style, where freshly washed and flat-ironed hair gives the smoothest finish.

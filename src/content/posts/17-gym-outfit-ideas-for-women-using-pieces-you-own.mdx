## Common Questions About Gym Style

### How do I choose the right colour palette for my gym clothes?

Start by selecting two or three neutral shades like sage, cream, or charcoal that you enjoy wearing. Neutrals are easier to mix and match than bright colours, which helps you create more outfit combinations with fewer items. Consider your gym environment and choose tones that make you feel focused.

### What fabrics are best for high intensity workouts?

Look for synthetic blends featuring nylon and spandex for their moisture wicking capabilities and stretch. These materials draw sweat away from the skin, helping you stay cool and dry. Avoid heavy cotton for intense sessions, as it absorbs moisture, becomes heavy, and takes a long time to dry out completely.

### How can I layer gym clothes for cold weather?

Layering is essential for maintaining comfort as your body warms up. Start with a fitted base layer like a tank or sports bra, then add a lightweight quarter zip or hoodie on top. This allows you to remove the outer layer easily once you begin your training and start sweating.

### What finishing details make an outfit look more polished?

Focus on the small elements like matching your socks to your trainers or choosing a cohesive colour for your accessories. A clean, matching set often looks more intentional than mismatched pieces. Ensure your clothing fits well, as proper sizing prevents bunching and creates a sleek, professional silhouette in the gym.

### How should I care for my activewear to keep it fresh?

Wash your gear on a cold, gentle cycle to preserve the elasticity of the synthetic fibres. Always skip the fabric softener, as it can coat the material and block its moisture wicking properties. Finally, hang dry your items to prevent heat damage and keep the fabric feeling soft and new.

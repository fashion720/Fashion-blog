Burgundy is the colour of winter. It is deep, warm and flattering on almost every skin tone, and it makes even a simple outfit look considered. The problem is that most people own one burgundy piece and never know what to pair it with, so it sits unworn. This guide gives you 17 complete burgundy outfits with exact formulas, so you can wear the shade from a Monday morning meeting to a Saturday night party without repeating a look.

Every outfit below is built for real winter weather, with warm fabrics, proper layering and footwear that handles cold pavements. You will also learn which colours pair with burgundy, when to wear it head to toe, and how a single burgundy accessory can rescue a neutral outfit. ## 1. Burgundy Wool Coat Over Neutrals

A long burgundy wool coat is the easiest way to wear the colour. Everything underneath stays neutral — cream knit, beige trousers, brown boots — so the coat does all the work. The result is elegant, warm and impossible to get wrong.

Formula: burgundy wool coat + cream knit sweater + beige wide-leg trousers + brown ankle boots.

The coat should hit below the knee for real warmth and a long, elegant line. Keep the knit thin enough to sit flat under the coat, and choose trousers in a warm beige rather than grey, which can fight the burgundy. A brown leather bag and gold jewellery suit the warm tones. This is the outfit for days when you want to look polished with almost no decisions to make.

## 2. Burgundy Knit Sweater and Dark Jeans

A burgundy chunky knit sweater with dark jeans is the cosy, everyday version of the trend. The sweater's texture makes the colour look richer, and dark denim keeps the outfit grounded. It is the look you will reach for most often this winter.

Formula: burgundy chunky knit sweater + dark wash skinny jeans + brown ankle boots.

Tuck the front of the sweater into the jeans to show your waist, and let the back stay loose. A thin thermal top underneath adds warmth without changing the shape. Brown boots warm up the palette; black boots make it sharper, so choose based on your mood. Add simple gold hoops and a tan crossbody bag. This outfit works for school runs, coffee dates and relaxed office days.

![A woman wearing a burgundy chunky knit sweater, dark wash skinny jeans and brown ankle boots in a cozy living room.](/images/content/17-rich-burgundy-winter-outfits-for-women/2.webp)

## 3. Burgundy Velvet Dress for Evenings

Velvet and burgundy were made for each other. A burgundy velvet midi dress catches the light, feels luxurious and needs almost no styling help. It is the dress for winter weddings, festive dinners and any evening where you want to look expensive.

Formula: burgundy velvet midi dress + gold drop earrings + nude or black closed-toe heels.

Keep the accessories warm-toned: gold jewellery, a small velvet clutch and heels in nude or black. A faux-fur stole over your shoulders solves the cold walk from the car, and sheer black tights add warmth without hiding the dress. Hang the dress the night before so the velvet falls smoothly. ## 4. Burgundy Leather Trousers and Cream Knit

Burgundy leather trousers are a statement piece that is easier to wear than it looks. Paired with a cream oversized knit, the outfit is soft on top and sharp below, and the colour contrast makes both pieces stand out. It is modern, warm and very photogenic.

Formula: burgundy leather trousers + cream oversized knit sweater + white sneakers.

The trousers should be straight-leg rather than skin-tight, or the look turns costume-like. Let the knit fall over the waistband and push the sleeves up slightly to show your wrists. White sneakers keep the outfit casual; swap them for heeled boots and it becomes an evening look. A cream or tan bag ties the top and bottom together.

![A woman wearing burgundy leather trousers with a cream oversized knit sweater and white sneakers in a modern bright interior.](/images/content/17-rich-burgundy-winter-outfits-for-women/4.webp)

## 5. Burgundy Midi Skirt and Turtleneck

A burgundy pleated midi skirt with a black turtleneck is elegant, warm and flattering. The pleats move beautifully, the turtleneck keeps you warm, and the dark top makes the skirt's colour glow. It is a refined choice for work and smart occasions.

Formula: burgundy pleated midi skirt + black fitted turtleneck + black tights + heeled ankle boots.

Choose a skirt that sits at your natural waist and falls below the knee. Black tights in at least 80 denier keep your legs warm, and heeled boots disappear under the hem. Add a thin belt in black or gold to define the waist. A structured black bag and simple stud earrings finish the look. This outfit also works with a cream turtleneck if you prefer a softer contrast.

![A woman wearing a burgundy pleated midi skirt, black turtleneck, black tights and heeled boots in a winter park.](/images/content/17-rich-burgundy-winter-outfits-for-women/5.webp)

## 6. Burgundy Blazer and Tailored Trousers

A burgundy blazer is the smartest way to wear the colour to work. It reads confident and polished, pairs with black, navy, cream and grey, and turns a plain white shirt into an outfit. Tailored black trousers keep the look professional.

Formula: burgundy blazer + white shirt + black tailored trousers + black loafers.

The blazer should fit at the shoulders with no pulling when buttoned. Roll the sleeves to the forearm for a relaxed feel, or keep them full length for formal days. A white shirt with a clean collar is essential; avoid busy prints that fight the blazer. Loafers keep it comfortable, while pointed heels make it sharper. ## 7. Burgundy Puffer Jacket for Cold Days

A burgundy puffer jacket proves that practical winter coats can still be stylish. The deep colour lifts a basic black leggings outfit, and the puffer's warmth means you can skip heavy layers underneath. It is the coat for the coldest, busiest days.

Formula: burgundy puffer jacket + black thermal top + black leggings + white trainers.

Keep the jacket hip-length or longer so it covers your waistband. The leggings should be fleece-lined for real warmth, and a thermal top underneath means you stay warm even when you take the jacket off indoors. White trainers add a fresh contrast; black trainers make the outfit darker and sleeker. Add a beanie in black or cream on freezing days.

## 8. Burgundy Satin Blouse and Black Trousers

A burgundy satin blouse is evening elegance with almost no effort. The fabric catches the light, the colour is rich, and black wide-leg trousers give it a long, graceful frame. It is the outfit for dinners, parties and date nights.

Formula: burgundy satin blouse + black wide-leg trousers + pointed black heels.

Tuck the blouse in fully and add a thin black belt to define the waist. The trousers should be high-waisted so the line stays long. Keep jewellery to one warm-toned piece, like gold hoops or a thin chain, so the satin stays the focus. A small black clutch completes the look. ## 9. Burgundy Scarf and Beanie Accents

You do not need a burgundy garment to wear burgundy. A burgundy scarf and beanie against a cream coat turn a plain neutral outfit into a styled one, and the accessories cost far less than a coat. This is the easiest entry point into the trend.

Formula: cream coat + burgundy chunky scarf + burgundy beanie + dark jeans + brown boots.

The scarf should be chunky and long enough to wrap twice; a thin scarf disappears against the coat. Match the beanie's shade to the scarf so the accents read intentional rather than random. Keep the rest of the outfit neutral so the burgundy pops. This trick works with any coat colour: navy, grey, black and camel all take burgundy accessories beautifully.

![A woman wearing a cream coat with a burgundy scarf, burgundy beanie and gloves in a snowy park.](/images/content/17-rich-burgundy-winter-outfits-for-women/9.webp)

## 10. Burgundy Corduroy Trousers and Denim Jacket

Burgundy corduroy trousers bring texture and warmth to a casual outfit. Paired with a classic denim jacket and a white t-shirt, the look is relaxed, retro and very wearable. Corduroy's soft pile makes the burgundy look even deeper.

Formula: burgundy corduroy trousers + denim jacket + white t-shirt + white sneakers.

The trousers should be straight-leg with a mid-rise waist for the most flattering fit. Wash corduroy inside out to protect the pile, and avoid tumble drying. A denim jacket in a classic mid-blue balances the warm burgundy; a black denim jacket makes the outfit darker. Add a tan leather belt and simple hoops. This is a great weekend outfit for markets and casual lunches.

![A woman wearing burgundy corduroy trousers with a denim jacket, white t-shirt and sneakers on a casual street.](/images/content/17-rich-burgundy-winter-outfits-for-women/10.webp)

## 11. Burgundy Maxi Dress with a Wool Coat

A burgundy maxi dress under a long camel wool coat is winter elegance at its best. The dress provides the colour, the coat provides the warmth and the length of both pieces creates a long, graceful line. It is the outfit for occasions that deserve something special.

Formula: burgundy maxi dress + long camel wool coat + heeled ankle boots.

The coat should be slightly longer than the dress, or the hem will peek out awkwardly. Heeled boots keep the dress off the ground and add height. A small structured bag in black or tan and gold jewellery finish the look. If the dress is sleeveless, a thin thermal top underneath is invisible and keeps you warm. This outfit suits winter weddings, festive dinners and smart evening events.

## 12. Burgundy Handbag as a Statement Piece

A burgundy handbag is the lowest-commitment way to wear the trend. Against a neutral beige outfit, one rich burgundy bag changes the whole look, drawing the eye and adding a point of colour exactly where you want it. It works with clothes you already own.

Formula: beige coat + beige knit + beige trousers + burgundy structured handbag + brown boots.

Keep the outfit tonal — beige, cream and camel — so the bag stands out cleanly. The bag should be structured rather than slouchy, because structure reads intentional. Match your shoes loosely to the bag's tone, or keep them neutral. This is the trick for anyone who loves the burgundy trend but does not want a burgundy garment: one accessory, worn often, does the job.

## 13. Burgundy and Camel Colour Blocking

Burgundy and camel are the warmest colour pairing of winter. A burgundy sweater with a camel coat and beige trousers looks expensive, coordinated and soft, with no black anywhere to harden the look. It is the combination to wear when you want to look polished without trying.

Formula: burgundy knit sweater + camel wool coat + beige wide-leg trousers + tan ankle boots.

Keep the tones warm throughout: cream, camel, tan and burgundy. Avoid cool greys, which clash with the warmth. The sweater should be visible at the neckline and cuffs so the burgundy frames the coat. A tan leather bag ties the palette together. This colour pairing photographs beautifully, which is why it dominates winter street style every year.

![A woman wearing a burgundy sweater with a camel coat and beige trousers, colour-blocked winter street style.](/images/content/17-rich-burgundy-winter-outfits-for-women/13.webp)

## 14. Burgundy Faux-Fur Coat for Glamour

A burgundy faux-fur coat is pure glamour. It is warm, dramatic and impossible to ignore, and it turns the simplest black dress into an evening outfit. Wear it when you want the coat to be the whole look.

Formula: burgundy faux-fur coat + black midi dress + black tights + heeled boots.

Keep everything underneath black and simple so the coat owns the outfit. The dress should be plain, the boots simple, the bag small and dark. Shake the faux fur out after wearing and store it in a cool, dry place to keep the pile soft. This is the coat for festive nights, holiday parties and evenings when you want to make an entrance.

## 15. Burgundy Jumpsuit for Winter Parties

A burgundy jumpsuit is the party outfit for people who hate dresses. It is comfortable, flattering and easy to layer: add a black blazer for warmth and structure, and heeled boots to lengthen the line. The single colour from top to bottom makes you look taller.

Formula: burgundy jumpsuit + black blazer + heeled ankle boots.

Choose a jumpsuit with a defined waist, either a wrap style or a belted one, so the shape does not disappear under the blazer. The blazer should be fitted, not oversized, or the outfit loses its line. Gold jewellery suits the warm tone. This is the outfit for office parties, festive dinners and nights when you want to dance without worrying about a hemline.

## 16. Burgundy Knee-High Boots with a Knit Dress

Burgundy knee-high boots are a statement that does the styling for you. With a cream knit dress and a camel coat, the boots add a rich flash of colour at the bottom of an otherwise neutral outfit. They are warm, practical and very eye-catching.

Formula: burgundy knee-high boots + cream knit dress + camel coat.

The boots should fit snugly at the calf so they stay up all day; if they slouch, they ruin the line. A knit dress in cream or oatmeal keeps the palette warm, and the camel coat ties the tones together. Keep the bag small and neutral. This outfit works for lunches, shopping days and any occasion where you want one bold detail without a full bold outfit.

## 17. Burgundy Monochrome Look from Head to Toe

A head-to-toe burgundy outfit is the boldest look on this list, and it works because the colour is deep enough to carry it. The secret is varying the textures: a wool coat, a knit sweater, smooth trousers and leather boots in slightly different burgundy shades create depth instead of a flat block.

Formula: burgundy wool coat + burgundy knit sweater + burgundy trousers + burgundy leather boots.

Vary the shades deliberately: a darker coat, a mid-tone sweater and slightly lighter trousers stop the outfit looking like a uniform. Mix textures too: wool, knit and leather each catch the light differently. Add one neutral accessory, like a cream scarf or a tan bag, to give the eye a resting point. This is the outfit for days when you want to feel unstoppable.

## Common Burgundy Styling Questions

### What colours go best with burgundy in winter?

Burgundy pairs best with cream, camel, black, navy and grey. Camel and cream warm it up, black makes it dramatic, and navy adds depth without competing. Avoid bright red, which clashes, and cool silver-grey, which can make burgundy look dull.

### Can I wear burgundy if I have warm skin undertones?

Yes, burgundy is one of the most flattering colours for warm undertones because it has red-brown warmth built in. If your skin is very fair, wear burgundy away from your face first, as a skirt or trousers, or add a cream scarf at the neckline to soften the contrast.

### Is burgundy still in style for winter 2026?

Yes, burgundy is one of the strongest colour trends of the season, with Pinterest searches for burgundy outfits rising steadily into winter. It appears across coats, knits, dresses and accessories, so it is a safe investment rather than a passing fad.

### How do I wear burgundy without looking too dark?

Pair burgundy with cream, camel or white to lift it. A cream knit under a burgundy coat, or a burgundy skirt with a cream top, keeps the outfit bright. Gold jewellery also warms burgundy up, while black accessories can make it heavier.

### What shoes go with burgundy outfits?

Brown ankle boots warm the palette up, black boots make it sharper, and nude heels work for evenings. Burgundy boots themselves are a statement: they need a neutral outfit around them so they stand out cleanly.

## Making Burgundy Work All Winter

Burgundy rewards repetition. Once you know your best pairings — camel for warmth, cream for brightness, black for drama — the colour becomes as easy as black, only richer. Start with one piece, a coat or a sweater, and build outward using the formulas above. Vogue's [winter outfit ideas](https://www.vogue.com/article/winter-outfit-ideas) show how deep seasonal colours like burgundy work across the whole winter wardrobe.

### Your Burgundy Winter Checklist

- Pick one hero burgundy piece first, then build neutrals around it
- Mix textures in monochrome looks so the colour has depth
- Choose gold jewellery over silver to keep the tones warm
- Keep a cream scarf handy to lift burgundy near your face
- Try the colour in an accessory first if a full garment feels too bold

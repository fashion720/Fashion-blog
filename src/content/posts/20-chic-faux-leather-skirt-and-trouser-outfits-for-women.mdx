Faux leather has quietly become the easiest way to look polished without trying very hard, and this list sticks to the two pieces that do the most work: skirts and trousers. A faux leather skirt brings instant structure to a soft knit, and faux leather trousers turn a plain sweater into an outfit that looks planned. You do not need real leather, and you do not need a big budget — most of these looks start with one faux leather piece and things you already own.

Below you will find 20 outfits, ten built around skirts and ten around trousers, each with the exact pieces, a formula you can copy, and a tip that saves you from the usual mistakes. You will learn which tops soften the shine, which shoes balance the weight of the fabric, and how to wear faux leather to work without it reading too loud. Start with the piece you already have, or use the checklist at the end before you shop.

## 1. The Black Faux Leather Pencil Skirt with a Cream Knit

A black faux leather pencil skirt with a cream chunky knit is the combination to reach for when you want to look dressed with almost no effort. The skirt brings the edge, the knit brings the softness, and together they balance each other out. Choose a skirt that sits at your natural waist and ends just below the knee — that length works with boots, heels, and flats, and it reads polished rather than party-only.

Tuck the front of the knit in so your waist shows, and finish with black ankle boots and a small crossbody bag. Gold hoops warm the whole look up. This is the outfit for lunch dates, the office on a casual Friday, and any day when jeans feel too plain. Black ankle boots are the finish that makes a leather skirt read winter-ready — our [17 stylish winter boots outfits](/posts/17-stylish-winter-boots-outfits-for-women/) show the pairs that work best with skirts like this one.

Formula: black faux leather pencil skirt + cream chunky knit + black ankle boots + small crossbody bag.

Tip: size the skirt so it sits flat at the waist with no pulling across the hips — faux leather does not stretch, so a snug fit will crease and gape all day.

### Your Faux Leather Buying and Care Checklist

Before you buy, or before you wear the piece you already own, run through this list:

- **Matte over glossy** — matte faux leather reads like real leather, while high-gloss reads plastic in daylight.
- **Flat at the waist** — faux leather does not stretch, so the waist must sit flat with no pulling when you sit.
- **Lined skirts** — a lined faux leather skirt does not cling to tights the way an unlined one does.
- **Wipe, don't wash** — wipe faux leather with a damp cloth after wear and never put it in the washing machine.
- **Hang, don't fold** — hang skirts and trousers so creases do not set into the surface.
- **One leather piece at a time** — a leather skirt with a leather top reads as a costume, not an outfit.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I wear faux leather to work?

Yes, if you keep the rest of the outfit tailored. A faux leather pencil or A-line skirt with a white shirt and heels reads as office-appropriate, and straight-leg faux leather trousers with a blouse work the same way. Skip minis, sequins, and glossy finishes at work, and keep the top structured rather than slinky.

### How do I stop faux leather from looking cheap?

Choose a matte finish, a clean cut with no extra zips or studs, and a fit that sits flat at the waist. Cheap-looking faux leather is usually glossy, thin, or badly cut. Pair it with quality fabrics — wool, silk, cotton — and the whole outfit reads more expensive than the skirt or trousers cost.

### What tops go best with a faux leather skirt?

Soft knits, silk blouses, and crisp shirts work best, because they contrast with the leather's structure. A chunky knit softens a pencil skirt, a silk blouse dresses up a slip skirt, and a white shirt makes a pleated skirt office-ready. Avoid pairing leather with another shiny fabric like satin trousers, which competes for attention.

### Are faux leather trousers warm enough for winter?

They block wind better than most fabrics, which makes them warmer than they look, but they do not insulate on their own. Wear a thermal base layer underneath on the coldest days, and pair them with a wool coat rather than a thin jacket. The windproof quality is a real advantage on cold, blustery days.

### How do I care for faux leather so it lasts?

Wipe it with a damp cloth after wear, hang it instead of folding it, and keep it away from direct heat and radiators, which can crack the surface. Never iron it directly or put it in the washing machine. Stored properly, a good faux leather piece lasts several seasons without peeling.

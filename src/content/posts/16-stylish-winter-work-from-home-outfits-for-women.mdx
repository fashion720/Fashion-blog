Working from home in winter is a balancing act. You want to feel warm and comfortable at your desk all day, but you also need to look presentable when a video call pops up without warning.

The trick is building outfits that are polished on top and cosy everywhere else. Soft knits, warm layers and a few smart finishing touches do the heavy lifting, so you can go from a morning of deep focus to an afternoon of back-to-back calls without changing.

## 1. The Chunky Cream Knit and Grey Joggers

A chunky cream knit is the fastest way to look put together at home. The texture reads as intentional on camera, even when the rest of the outfit is pure comfort.

Pair it with soft grey knit joggers and beige fuzzy slippers. The joggers stay out of frame on video calls, and the slippers keep your feet warm on cold floors. Add a simple gold necklace so the top half looks finished. ## 2. The Camel Wrap Cardigan and White Tee

A camel wrap cardigan is a work-from-home hero because it behaves like a blazer on camera but feels like a blanket. The wrap shape defines your waist, and the warm tone flatters most skin tones on screen.

Layer it over a plain white tee with black leggings and tan ankle boots. If a call comes in, you only need to sit up straight. The boots matter for the school run or a coffee break, when the full outfit is on show.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What should I wear for a winter video call at home?

A polished top half matters most: a turtleneck, a smart blouse or a structured cardigan in a colour that suits you. Keep the camera at eye level and choose solid colours over busy prints, which can shimmer on screen.

### How do I look polished on camera without giving up comfort?

Build the outfit around one structured piece, like a blazer, a wrap cardigan or a crisp shirt, and keep everything else soft. Add small earrings and tidy hair, and the camera will do the rest.

### Are leggings and joggers acceptable for working from home?

Yes, as long as the top half looks intentional. Pair them with a long knit, a structured cardigan or a blazer, and choose thick, opaque fabrics rather than thin gym leggings.

### How can I stay warm at my desk all day without piling on layers?

Start with a thermal base layer under your knit, keep your feet warm with thick socks or shearling slippers, and drape a shawl or blanket over your lap. Warm feet make the whole body feel warmer.

### What colours work best on camera for winter video calls?

Rich, warm tones like burgundy, forest green, camel and plum read clearly on screen. Avoid stark white, which can glare, and very pale pastels, which can wash you out in winter light.

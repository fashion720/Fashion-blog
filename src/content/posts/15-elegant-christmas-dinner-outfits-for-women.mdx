Christmas dinner is the one night of the year when the table is dressed up, the candles are lit, and everyone notices what you are wearing. This guide gives you 15 elegant Christmas dinner outfits for women, each with a step-by-step plan and a ready-to-copy outfit formula, so you can stop second-guessing and enjoy the evening.

Every look below is designed for a real dinner table: comfortable for three courses, polished for family photos, and warm for a December evening. Whether your dinner is a formal sit-down, a cozy family gathering, or a glamorous party, you will find an outfit here that fits the dress code.

## 1. Classic Velvet Midi Dress

![Woman in a burgundy velvet midi dress at a Christmas dinner](/images/content/15-elegant-christmas-dinner-outfits-for-women/1.webp)

Nothing says Christmas dinner quite like velvet. A burgundy velvet midi dress is the safest elegant choice in this guide — the fabric does the heavy lifting: it looks rich, photographs beautifully in candlelight, and feels soft and warm on a cold December night.

Start with a long-sleeve or three-quarter-sleeve midi in burgundy, forest green, or deep navy. Choose an A-line or gently fitted silhouette that skims your waist without clinging, since you will be sitting for hours. Add nude or black pointed-toe heels, a delicate gold necklace, and small stud earrings. Keep your bag small — a clutch or a tiny crossbody that hangs on your chair.

Formula: Burgundy velvet midi dress + nude pointed heels + gold jewelry + small clutch.

**Tip:** Velvet creases where you sit, so stand and smooth the skirt before photos, and skip a heavy belt that would press a line into the fabric.

## 2. Satin Slip Dress and Blazer

![Woman in a champagne satin slip dress with a cream blazer](/images/content/15-elegant-christmas-dinner-outfits-for-women/2.webp)

A satin slip dress softened by a tailored blazer is the perfect balance of glamorous and grown-up. The slip dress brings the shine, while the blazer keeps the look dinner-appropriate and adds warmth for the walk to the table.

Pick a champagne, gold, or deep green slip dress in a bias cut that falls just below the knee. Layer a cream or camel blazer over it, unbuttoned so the satin still shows. Add strappy gold heels, a small clutch, and one statement piece — a cuff bracelet or drop earrings, not both. If the room warms up, slip the blazer off your shoulders during dessert and let the dress take over.

Formula: Champagne satin slip dress + cream blazer + gold strappy heels + one statement jewel.

**Tip:** Satin highlights every lump and bump, so wear smooth, seamless underwear and do a quick sit-test in the mirror before you leave.

## 3. Burgundy Wrap Dress

![Woman in a burgundy wrap dress at a festive dinner](/images/content/15-elegant-christmas-dinner-outfits-for-women/3.webp)

The wrap dress is the most forgiving elegant silhouette ever designed, and burgundy is the most festive color you can wear without a single sequin. Together they flatter almost every body shape and let you eat comfortably.

Choose a true wrap or faux-wrap dress with a V-neckline and three-quarter sleeves, hitting just below the knee. Tie the waist sash at your natural waist — not too tight — and let the skirt fall in soft folds. Pair it with black pointed-toe heels, pearl or gold studs, and a small black clutch. Soft waves in your hair and a berry lip finish the look.

Formula: Burgundy wrap dress + black pointed heels + pearl studs + berry lipstick.

**Tip:** Pin the wrap overlap with a small safety pin at the bust so the neckline stays put through dinner and photos.

## 4. Black Sequin Top and Tailored Trousers

![Woman in a black sequin top and tailored black trousers](/images/content/15-elegant-christmas-dinner-outfits-for-women/4.webp)

If a full dress is not your style, a sequin top with tailored trousers gives you the same festive sparkle with more freedom to move. It is also one of the best Christmas dinner outfits for women who want to look dressed up without feeling overdressed at a family table.

Start with a black sequin top — a short- or long-sleeve blouse, or a fitted camisole under a sheer-sleeve top. Tuck it into high-waisted black tailored trousers with a clean, straight leg. Add black pointed heels, a velvet clutch, and simple gold hoops. The sequins are the star, so keep everything else matte and understated.

Formula: Black sequin top + high-waisted black trousers + pointed heels + velvet clutch.

**Tip:** Sequin tops shed. Shake yours gently outside before you put it on, and carry a lint roller for a quick touch-up after the car ride.

## 5. Emerald Green Jumpsuit

![Woman in an emerald green jumpsuit at a Christmas dinner](/images/content/15-elegant-christmas-dinner-outfits-for-women/5.webp)

An emerald green jumpsuit is the modern answer to the Christmas dinner dress — one piece, zero matching stress, and a color that looks stunning against candlelight and evergreen decor. Wide-leg cuts also make it one of the most comfortable options here.

Look for a jumpsuit with a defined waist — a built-in tie or a sewn-in seam — and wide legs that skim the floor over heels. A V-neck or wrap bodice keeps the top half elegant. Add gold strappy heels, a gold clutch, and gold drop earrings. Keep your hair swept up or to one side so the neckline stays visible.

Formula: Emerald wide-leg jumpsuit + gold strappy heels + gold clutch + gold drop earrings.

**Tip:** Try the jumpsuit sitting down before the night — if the waistband digs in, size up or choose a style with a stretch waist panel.

## 6. Cashmere Sweater and Pleated Skirt

![Woman in a cream cashmere sweater and pleated gold midi skirt](/images/content/15-elegant-christmas-dinner-outfits-for-women/6.webp)

For a Christmas dinner at home or a relaxed family gathering, a cashmere sweater with a pleated skirt is elegant without trying too hard. It is warm, soft, and comfortable, yet the metallic pleats give it a festive glow a plain sweater never could.

Choose a fine-knit cream, ivory, or soft grey cashmere sweater and tuck it into a pleated metallic midi skirt in gold, champagne, or silver. Add nude block heels, a small top-handle bag, and a delicate pendant necklace. French-tuck the front of the sweater so your waistline shows, and push the sleeves to the forearm for a relaxed, polished finish.

Formula: Cream cashmere sweater + pleated metallic midi skirt + nude block heels + pendant necklace.

## 7. Gold Metallic Blouse and Black Pants

![Woman in a gold metallic blouse and black tailored pants](/images/content/15-elegant-christmas-dinner-outfits-for-women/7.webp)

A gold metallic blouse is a one-piece shortcut to festive. Paired with perfectly tailored black pants, it reads as intentional and elegant rather than flashy, and it works for a restaurant dinner as well as a party at a friend's house.

Pick a gold blouse in a soft metallic finish — not mirror-shine — with a relaxed fit and long or draped sleeves. Tuck it into high-waisted black pants with a straight or slightly tapered leg. Add black pointed-toe heels, a black clutch, and simple studs. A red lip is the only extra color you need, and it ties the whole look to Christmas.

Formula: Gold metallic blouse + black tailored pants + black heels + red lipstick.

**Tip:** Metallic fabric can look cheap in harsh light, so check your blouse under warm indoor lighting, and choose matte black pants to balance the shine.

## 8. Lace Detail Midi Dress

![Woman in a navy lace detail midi dress at a Christmas dinner](/images/content/15-elegant-christmas-dinner-outfits-for-women/8.webp)

Lace is quietly romantic, and a lace detail midi dress brings soft, feminine elegance to the Christmas table without a single sparkle. It is a lovely choice if your dinner leans traditional and you want to look refined rather than flashy.

Choose a navy, black, or deep wine dress with lace on the sleeves, bodice, or hem — full lace feels heavy, so detail panels flatter more. A fitted bodice with a slightly flared skirt suits most figures. Pair it with silver or nude heels, a small silver clutch, and stud earrings. Keep makeup soft and hair in a low bun or loose waves so the lace stays the focus.

Formula: Lace detail midi dress + silver heels + small clutch + soft waves.

**Tip:** Lace snags easily on jewelry and rough table edges, so skip chunky bracelets with this dress and watch out for rings with raised stones. ## 9. Tartan Dress with a Belt

![Woman in a red tartan plaid dress with a black belt](/images/content/15-elegant-christmas-dinner-outfits-for-women/9.webp)

Tartan is the print of Christmas. A red or green tartan dress with a belt looks festive the moment you put it on, and the belt gives the plaid structure so the look stays elegant, not casual.

Pick a tartan dress in red, green, or black watch plaid, fit-and-flare or shift, hitting at or just below the knee. Add a slim black leather belt at your natural waist. Wear black tights and black ankle boots or pointed heels, depending on how formal your dinner is. A small black bag and gold studs complete it — the print is busy enough on its own.

Formula: Tartan fit-and-flare dress + slim black belt + black tights + black ankle boots.

**Tip:** Match the scale of the plaid to your frame: larger checks suit taller figures, while a smaller tartan reads better on petite frames. ## 10. Cream Silk Blouse and Wide-Leg Trousers

![Woman in a cream silk blouse and ivory wide-leg trousers](/images/content/15-elegant-christmas-dinner-outfits-for-women/10.webp)

Cream on cream is one of the most quietly luxurious looks you can wear to Christmas dinner. A silk blouse with wide-leg trousers feels soft, moves beautifully, and stands out because it is not red, green, or sparkly.

Start with a cream or ivory silk blouse with a soft collar or a small bow at the neck. Tuck it into high-waisted wide-leg trousers in ivory or soft champagne. Add nude pointed heels, a gold pendant necklace, and a small structured bag in cream or tan. Keep makeup warm and natural — a soft brown eye and a nude lip — so the outfit's glow does the talking.

Formula: Cream silk blouse + ivory wide-leg trousers + nude heels + gold pendant.

**Tip:** Silk stains fast, so eat carefully and keep a stain-remover pen in your clutch. If red wine is on the menu, swap the cream trousers for a darker pair and keep the silk top.

## 11. Red Velvet Blazer and Jeans

Formula: Red velvet blazer + black camisole + dark straight-leg jeans + black heeled boots.

## Before You Sit Down: A Quick Christmas Dinner Checklist

- **Comfort test:** Sit, stand, and reach in your outfit before you leave — if anything pinches or slips, fix it now.
- **Shoes:** Choose heels you can stand in for an hour, or pack foldable flats for the drive home.
- **Bag check:** Phone, lipstick, stain-remover pen, safety pin, and a lint roller — that is all you need.
- **Photos:** Do a quick mirror check after the first course, when hair and makeup need a refresh.
- **Warmth:** Bring a wrap, blazer, or coat layer even if the venue promises heating.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What should I wear to a Christmas dinner at someone's home?

Wear something elegant but comfortable, like a velvet midi dress, a wrap dress, or a cashmere sweater with a pleated skirt. Home dinners are usually less formal than restaurant dinners, so skip floor-length gowns and heavy sequins. Choose an outfit you can sit in for hours and eat in comfortably.

### Is it okay to wear red to Christmas dinner?

Yes, red is one of the best colors for Christmas dinner, as long as you keep the rest of the outfit simple. A burgundy wrap dress, a red velvet blazer, or red heels with a black dress all look festive without trying too hard. If your host wears red too, your silhouette and accessories will still set you apart.

### Can I wear jeans to an elegant Christmas dinner?

Jeans can work for a casual family Christmas dinner if they are dark-wash, straight-leg, and rip-free — especially with a velvet blazer or a silk blouse and heeled boots. For a formal or restaurant dinner, tailored trousers or a dress are a safer choice. When in doubt, ask your host how formal the evening will be.

### What shoes are best for a Christmas dinner outfit?

Pointed-toe heels in black or nude suit most Christmas dinner outfits, and block heels are the comfortable alternative if you will be on your feet a lot. Avoid stilettos higher than three inches unless you walk confidently in them. Heeled boots work well for home dinners and cold evenings — always pack foldable flats for the journey home.

### How can I make my Christmas dinner outfit look more elegant?

Fit and finish matter more than price. Steam or iron your outfit, choose one focal piece like velvet or sequins, and keep the rest simple. Add polished details: good lipstick, neat hair, clean shoes, and a small structured bag.

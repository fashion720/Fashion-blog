## Senior Shirt Questions, Answered for the Class of 2027

**Q1: What shirt do you wear for senior class of 2027 photos?**&#x20;

A1: Pick a shirt with clean text or a small graphic, not a busy print that competes with your face. Embroidered patches or hand painted florals photograph well since the detail reads clearly. Avoid neon colours or heavy glitter, since both can wash out under camera flash or bright sun.

**Q2: How do you style an oversized senior shirt without looking sloppy?**&#x20;

A2: Tuck the front into high rise jeans or shorts to define your waist. A half tuck works too, and it keeps the relaxed fit intentional. Add a belt if the shirt runs long. Rolled sleeves and simple jewellery finish the look without adding bulk.

**Q3: Can you wear a senior shirt to a job interview or formal event?**&#x20;

A3: Skip it for anything formal. Senior shirts belong at tailgates, senior trips, class parties, and casual school events. For graduation ceremonies themselves, check your school's dress code first, since many require solid colours under gowns rather than printed tees.

**Q4: What is the best fabric for a senior shirt worn all year?**&#x20;

A4: Cotton and cotton blends hold up best across seasons and washes without pilling. Waffle knit sweaters add warmth for fall, while lightweight jersey tees suit spring and summer events. Mesh jerseys breathe well for outdoor games but run thin, so layer them when temperatures drop.

**Q5: How much should a senior class shirt cost?**&#x20;

A5: Most quality senior shirts run between twenty and forty-five dollars, depending on fabric and detail work like embroidery or chenille patches. Basic screen-printed tees sit at the lower end. Shirts with hand painted or embroidered designs cost more due to the extra labour involved.

**Q6: When should you buy your senior year shirt?**&#x20;

A6: Shop early fall, right when the school year starts, so you have options for tailgates and pep rallies through winter. Order graduation specific shirts by early spring, since custom or embroidered pieces often need extra production time before ceremonies in May or June.
